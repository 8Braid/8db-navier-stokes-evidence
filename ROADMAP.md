# What we want to establish next

The public companion is a bounded starting point: a checked algebraic lemma, exact examples, an interactive explanation and replay records. This roadmap describes proposed work. Items below are not claims of completed capabilities.

## First: test whether the explanation helps

Invite researchers and developers of mathematical AI to predict what happens when an assumption changes, identify the relevant premise, and find the supporting formal statement. Compare their answers before and after using the companion. Record errors and task completion, including cases where the explanation misleads. No comprehension or productivity improvement has yet been measured.

An external reproduction of the existing Lean checks would add a different kind of evidence. Record the source revision, environment and actual output, and distinguish an unaffiliated reproduction from our own archived run.

## Then: apply the method to a second problem

Select a small public example with explicit assumptions and a checkable conclusion. It should include a change that removes one sufficient guarantee and, if available, a separate exact obstruction. Publish the problem, checks and explanation so another person can reproduce the distinction.

The adoption question is concrete: can a researcher bring their own example without depending on a custom demonstration built by us? General certificate interfaces, clear reuse terms and simpler environment setup are part of that work. A second example alone would not establish a universal proof engine.

## The broader 8DB direction

The intended research workflow connects a claim to complete groups of premises, derivations and verification evidence. A change should make the affected support visible while preserving independently valid alternatives. Publicly demonstrating that behavior with the native engine requires its own reproducible package and qualification. The present browser companion illustrates the mathematical distinction without executing that database workflow.

## The Navier-Stokes research frontier

A substantive target in the separate research program is an explicit open, infinite-dimensional family around a dynamically corrected flow that reaches a known globally regular region. A complete proof would need a consistent moving-core derivative, control of dangerous transverse modes, a uniform theorem for every remaining frequency, and a verified continuation handoff.

Finite polynomial identities and successful matrix checks can support that argument. They do not establish those continuous PDE obligations by themselves. This public release contains no complete proof of that tube and no resolution of the general unforced three-dimensional problem.

## Suggest a useful case

[Submit a changed-assumption example](https://github.com/8Braid/8db-navier-stokes-evidence/issues/new?template=changed-assumption.md). State the original claim, its assumptions, the proposed change and the decision it would help you make. We will use the examples to assess relevance and choose a bounded next experiment. Submitting an example does not imply a review or implementation commitment.
